GENERAL SYNTAX
- Format: MIDICode-Duration-Velocity (Example: 36-1-127)[cite: 8].
- MIDICode: Standard General MIDI (GM) percussion number[cite: 8].
- Duration: Beat length before the next event (e.g., 0.5 = 8th note)[cite: 8].
- Velocity: Hit strength (0-127), which is crucial for dynamic drumming[cite: 8].

DRUM PRINCIPLES & USAGE
- Common General MIDI Drum Map[cite: 8]:
  * 36: Bass Drum (Kick 1)[cite: 8]
  * 38: Acoustic Snare[cite: 8]
  * 42: Closed Hi-Hat[cite: 8]
  * 43: High Tom[cite: 8]
  * 45: Low Tom[cite: 8]
  * 46: Open Hi-Hat[cite: 8]
  * 49: Crash Cymbal 1[cite: 8]
  * 51: Ride Cymbal 1[cite: 8]
- Multiple Hits (Simultaneous): Enclose drum codes in brackets '[...]' to hit them exactly at the same time[cite: 8]. Example: Kick + Crash is [36,49]-1-127, and Snare + Hi-hat is [38,42]-0.5-100[cite: 8].
- Ghost Notes & Accents: Manipulate the Velocity parameter to create realistic human grooves[cite: 8]. An Accent (Hard hit) is 38-1-127, and a Ghost Note (Soft tap) is 38-0.25-30[cite: 8].
