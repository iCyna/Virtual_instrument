--- PIANO FULL EXAMPLE & USAGE ---
Goal: Play a C Major chord for 2 beats, then an F Major chord for 2 beats.

1. SEQUENCE CODE:
[C4,E4,G4]-2-100 [F4,A4,C5]-2-100

2. EXPLANATION:
- Standard musical pitches are used and wrapped in brackets `[]` to form chords[cite: 1].
- The sequence strictly follows the Note-Beat-Velocity format[cite: 1].
- `[C4,E4,G4]-2-100` plays the C Major chord for exactly 2 beats at a velocity of 100[cite: 1].

3. HOW TO USE IN MTE:
- Open the "Advanced Step Sequencer (Notes/Chords)" menu[cite: 1].
- Select "Piano"[cite: 1].
- Paste the sequence code above and press Enter. The tracker will render it in the background[cite: 1].
